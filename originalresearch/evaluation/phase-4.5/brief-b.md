# Brief B — the arts venue

Illustrative draft created during preparation. No real client/project provenance is recorded; excluded as an accepted real brief until that requirement is satisfied. Text retained as historical preparation evidence.

> We run a 90-seat independent theatre above a pub in Brunswick. We program about four shows a
> season, plus late-night comedy and the occasional gig. Our audience is 25 to 45, students and
> locals, and they mostly look at the site on a phone in a noisy bar.
>
> What I need: what is on this week, how to book, how to get here, and whether there is anything
> accessible about the building. I want the current show to be the loudest thing on the page and
> the rest to get out of the way. We sell tickets through a link to our ticketing provider, so the
> site does not need a shop.
>
> We have a photographer who shoots every show, so we have dark, grainy, beautiful photographs.
> I want them used properly. I do not want a purple gradient, a carousel, or a "buy tickets now"
> button that covers the photo. I like the way the Design Museum and the Barbican sites feel —
> confident and a bit odd, not corporate.
