# Brief A — the local trade

Illustrative draft created during preparation. No real client/project provenance is recorded; excluded as an accepted real brief until that requirement is satisfied. Text retained as historical preparation evidence.

> I run a two-person plumbing and gas-fitting business in the inner north of Melbourne. I want a
> simple site that gets me phone calls, not a brochure. Jobs I want: blocked drains, hot water
> systems, gas fitting, and emergency call-outs. I am licensed, insured, and I answer my own phone.
> Customers are mostly homeowners aged 35 to 60, plus a few real-estate managers.
>
> What I need on it: what I do, the areas I cover, proof I am licensed, my phone number visible at
> all times on a phone, and a short form. I have four photos of finished jobs, not great ones.
> I do not want stock photos of smiling men in hard hats, and I do not want a red-and-blue
> "emergency" site that looks like every other plumber.
>
> I like things that look calm and well made, the way a good café menu does. My colours are
> probably white, a dark charcoal and one warm colour. Please do not make it look like a template.
