# Design taste is a core part of version one

Cauldron is not just a visual Astro builder. Built-in design taste (taste rules, a design direction per site, curated sections, and quality and critique checks) is one of version one's three pillars, alongside visual editing and the agent. It carries on from Cauldron OS, whose taste layer is what set it apart. It's built alongside the editor, in place before the agent's first real job, and version one doesn't ship until it is proven to work. Deferring taste to a later release was rejected: it is the differentiator. Decided by Billy, 6 Nov 2026.
